# G4 — Team + SSO

Branch: `reef-control-plane`

## Delivered

- Team and team-member DB records across SQLite and Postgres adapters.
- `POST /v1/sso/login` uses the existing OIDC discovery/token-flow helper against a local dev IdP.
- SSO login creates or updates account, license, quota, team, and member records.
- Signed JWTs from SSO carry the team tenant id.
- `GET /v1/team/audit` enforces team membership and returns DB-backed team usage.
- SSO, team membership, team audit, and team audit denial are gateway evidence links.

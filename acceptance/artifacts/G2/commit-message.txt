G2: enforce quota and local billing ledger

Add DB-backed quota enforcement, honest quota reporting, cost records, and a
billing adapter interface. The default local billing adapter writes to the DB
billing ledger; the Stripe adapter is explicitly stubbed and throws rather than
pretending to bill.

Completion requests now evidence-log quota decisions before routing, deny
over-quota callers fail-closed, debit actual provider usage, and expose sourced
quota numbers through GET /v1/quota.
